# Nixorium Trademark Policy

Nixorium is free software. You can use it, share it, study it and change it.
This policy is about something different: the name and the logo. It exists so
that "Nixorium" always points to the official project, and nobody is misled
about who made a release or who supports a lab.

## You can do this without asking

- Refer to Nixorium truthfully: in articles, talks, lessons, school documents,
  tenders and procurement documents, reviews and comparisons.
- Say that your lab or school uses Nixorium.
- Distribute the unmodified official releases under the Nixorium name.
- Name a compatible tool or guide in a way that makes clear it is separate,
  such as "X for Nixorium" or "works with Nixorium".
- Use the unmodified logo to link to [nixorium.org](https://nixorium.org) or
  to the GitHub project.
- Modify Nixorium privately, inside your own school or organization.

## You need written permission for this

- Naming a fork, a modified distribution, a hosted or paid service, a product
  or a company "Nixorium" or something confusingly similar.
- Using the name or the logo in a domain name, an app or package name, or a
  social media account.
- Offering paid support, training or installation under the Nixorium name in a
  way that suggests it is official. You may say truthfully that you provide
  services for Nixorium, as long as you also say that you are independent.
- Selling merchandise that carries the name or the logo.

## You must never do this

- Suggest an endorsement, partnership or certification that does not exist.
- Use the name or the logo on modified software while presenting it as the
  official release.
- Alter the logo.
- Use the name or the logo in a misleading or unlawful way.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the MIT License. Give a modified distribution a
different name and a different logo. You may say that it is "based on
Nixorium" or "a fork of Nixorium".

## How to write and show the name

- Spell it "Nixorium", with a capital N.
- Do not combine it with your own brand into a new name.
- Keep the logo's proportions and colors.

## Questions and permission

Write to [hello@nixorium.org](mailto:hello@nixorium.org) to ask for permission
or to ask a question. Please say what you plan to do and where the name will
appear.

This policy may change. The version in the main branch of the repository is
the current one.
